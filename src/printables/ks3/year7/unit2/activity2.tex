\begin{SGSCallout}{What you are learning}

\begin{SGSStep}{3}{Complete the Missions (What to look for)}
As you complete the missions, focus on these key checks:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,label=$\square$]
  \item \textbf{Browser:} Look at the cookie banner. What is the difference between \textit{Necessary} and \textit{All} cookies?
  \item \textbf{Connectify:} Read the permissions carefully. Does a social app really need your location \textit{all the time}? Check the Terms \& Conditions for the \textit{data retention} rule.
  \item \textbf{MapMe:} Compare \textit{While Using App} vs \textit{Always Allow}. Why would an app want to track you when you are not using it?
  \item \textbf{SkyWatch:} Try \textit{Approximate Location}. Do you still get the weather? What is the privacy benefit?
\end{itemize}
\end{SGSStep}
