📝 Exercise book tasks

Pick cards in the results screen (tap Pick), then answer these prompts in your exercise book.

↓ Not OK: None yet
← Use your name: None yet
→ Stay anonymous: None yet
↑ It depends: None yet
1 From "Not OK Online" pile

Choose 1 card. Write 3 short sentences:

  • What's the harm?
  • Who could get hurt?
  • What should the person do instead?
2 From "Good & Use Your Name" pile

Choose 1 card. Write 3 short sentences:

  • Why is this positive?
  • Why is it OK to use your name here?
  • How does being yourself help the situation?
3 From "Good but Stay Anonymous" pile

Choose 1 card. Write 3 short sentences:

  • Why is this OK?
  • What could go wrong if you used your real name?
  • What does anonymity protect (privacy, safety, feelings)?
4 From "It Depends…" pile

Choose 3 cards. For each card, write:

  • What makes it tricky?
  • One reason it might be OK, and one reason it might not.
  • What rule would you follow to stay safe and respectful?
