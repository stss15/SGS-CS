Introduction to Computing

Purpose: A fast revision guide for Unit 1. Use it to refresh tools, terms, and workflows before lessons and assessments.

In this unit you will learn
  • How to handle and operate your MacBook safely and confidently.
  • How to navigate macOS, Finder, and common windows.
  • How to organize files and use OneDrive for schoolwork.
  • How to use core shortcuts for speed and accuracy.
  • How to choose the right app for the right task.
  • How to communicate and collaborate with Teams, email, and OneNote.
Concepts Covered
Adaptation
How people and systems adjust to new tools and workflows.
In this unit: you adapt to the school device setup and daily routines.
Form
How information is organized and structured.
In this unit: you build clear folder structures and tidy notebooks.
Function
The purpose of tools and systems.
In this unit: you learn what each app is best used for.
Skills Developed
Digital literacy
Use devices and apps safely and efficiently.
Data handling
Name, save, and organize files clearly.
Communication
Send clear emails and messages.
Presentation
Choose the right format for ideas.

1.1 MacBook Basics

Know the hardware, core gestures, and safe login and power habits.

1.1.1 Ports, Charging, Safety

Keywords
KeywordDefinition
USB-CPort for charging and accessories.
MagSafeMagnetic charger on some models.
Power buttonKey used to turn on the MacBook.
Headphone jackRound socket for audio output.
  • Charge using USB-C (or MagSafe if your model has it).
  • Check the charging light before unplugging.
  • Carry with two hands and keep the lid closed.

1.1.2 Trackpad Basics

Keywords
KeywordDefinition
TrackpadTouch surface for moving the cursor.
ClickTap or press to select.
Right clickTwo-finger tap for options.
ScrollTwo-finger move to travel on a page.
  • Single click to select, double click to open.
  • Two-finger tap opens extra options (right click).
  • Two-finger scroll moves pages up and down.
GestureAction
One-finger tapSelect or click.
Two-finger tapOpen options menu.
Two-finger scrollMove through a page.

1.1.3 Login, Power, Security

Keywords
KeywordDefinition
LoginSign in with your school account.
PasswordPrivate text used for access.
SleepLow power state with work ready.
Shut downPower off completely.
  • Passwords show as dots to stay private.
  • Sleep is quick for short breaks; shut down for longer gaps.
  • Save work before sleeping or shutting down.
Power stateBest used for
SleepShort breaks between lessons.
Shut downEnd of day or travel home.

1.2 Typing and Keyboard Shortcuts

Build accuracy first, then speed. Use shortcuts to save time in every subject.

1.2.1 Posture and Home Row

Keywords
KeywordDefinition
PostureHow you sit and position your body.
Home rowASDF JKL; keys where fingers rest.
AccuracyTyping the correct letters.
  • Feet flat, back straight, elbows near 90 degrees.
  • Find the bumps on F and J to locate the home row.
  • Accuracy comes first; speed follows.

1.2.2 Core Shortcuts

Keywords
KeywordDefinition
Command (Cmd)Main shortcut key on a Mac.
CopyDuplicate selected content.
PasteInsert copied content.
UndoReverse your last action.
  • Hold Cmd and tap the letter key.
  • Shortcuts work in most apps and save time.
ShortcutAction
Cmd + CCopy
Cmd + VPaste
Cmd + XCut
Cmd + ASelect all
Cmd + ZUndo
Cmd + SSave

1.2.3 Screenshots and Clipboard

Keywords
KeywordDefinition
ScreenshotImage of your screen.
ClipboardTemporary storage for copied items.
RedoUndo the undo (Cmd + Shift + Z).
  • Screenshots save to the Desktop by default.
  • Clipboard holds one copied item at a time.
  • Use Undo and Redo to fix mistakes quickly.
ShortcutCapture
Cmd + Shift + 3Full screen
Cmd + Shift + 4Selected area
Cmd + Shift + 4 then SpaceSpecific window

1.3 Finder and File Management

Find, organize, and protect your files using Finder and OneDrive.

1.3.1 Finder and Navigation

Keywords
KeywordDefinition
FinderApp for browsing files and folders.
DockRow of app icons at the bottom.
Menu barTop bar that changes per app.
  • Finder is the blue smiley face icon.
  • Keep the Desktop clean; store work in folders.
  • Use the menu bar for the active app options.

1.3.2 OneDrive and Sync

Keywords
KeywordDefinition
OneDriveSchool cloud storage.
SyncFile updates between device and cloud.
Local storageFiles saved only on your MacBook.
  • Save all school work to OneDrive.
  • Check sync status before closing your MacBook.
  • Ask for help if you see a red sync error.
StatusMeaning
Green checkSynced and safe.
Spinning arrowsSyncing in progress.
Red XSync error - tell a teacher.

1.3.3 Folders, Naming, Search

Keywords
KeywordDefinition
FolderContainer for organizing files.
Naming conventionConsistent, clear file names.
Quick LookPreview file with the space bar.
  • Use a simple hierarchy: Year7 > Subject > Topic.
  • Name files clearly, avoid symbols like / and ?.
  • Use Search and Quick Look to find work fast.

1.4 Apps and Window Control

Open apps quickly and manage windows so your workspace stays focused.

1.4.1 Dock, Launchpad, Spotlight

Keywords
KeywordDefinition
DockBar of app icons at the bottom.
LaunchpadGrid of all apps.
SpotlightSearch for apps and files quickly.
  • Pin your most used apps in the Dock.
  • Use Launchpad to find apps by category.
  • Use Spotlight (Cmd + Space) for fast search.

1.4.2 Window Controls and Split View

Keywords
KeywordDefinition
Window controlsRed, yellow, green buttons.
Split ViewTwo apps side by side.
Full screenApp fills the display.
  • Red closes, yellow minimizes, green enters full screen.
  • Hold green to choose Split View.
  • Use Mission Control to see all windows at once.

1.4.3 Force Quit and Troubleshooting

Keywords
KeywordDefinition
Force QuitClose an unresponsive app.
FreezeApp stops responding.
RestartTurn off and back on safely.
  • Cmd + Option + Esc opens the Force Quit menu.
  • Force Quit is for frozen apps only.
  • Restart if the whole system is slow or stuck.

Choose the right tool and keep work clean and consistent.

Keywords
KeywordDefinition
HeadingTitle that organizes sections.
BulletsShort, easy-to-scan points.
LayoutHow text and images are arranged.
  • Use headings to show structure.

1.5.3 Export and Submit

Keywords
KeywordDefinition

1.6 Email Basics

Write clear, respectful emails and attach work correctly.

1.6.1 Email Structure

Keywords
KeywordDefinition
Subject lineShort summary of the email.
GreetingPolite opening.
Sign-offPolite closing.
  • Use a clear subject, e.g., "Unit 1 Homework".

1.6.2 Attachments and Links

Keywords
KeywordDefinition

1.7 OneNote Navigation

Keywords
KeywordDefinition
NotebookThe full OneNote file.
SectionDivider inside a notebook.
PageSingle note within a section.

Keywords
KeywordDefinition

1.7.3 Sync, Search, Tags

Keywords
KeywordDefinition
