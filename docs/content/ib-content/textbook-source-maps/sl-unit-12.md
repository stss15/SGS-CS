Unit Plan: SL Unit 12 - Case Study Inquiry
Subject: IB Computer Science (First Assessment 2027)
Grade: Year 13 (SL)
Duration: 3 Weeks (9 Lessons)
Theme: Case Study (Paper 1, Section B)
Unit Foundation
Concepts The core concept is Strategic Evaluation of Emerging Technology. This unit requires students to move from general syllabus knowledge to the investigative research of a specific, pre-seen scenario. Key themes include System Interdependence, Contextual Application, and the Justification of Solutions in real-world emergencies or developments.
Content
	•	Understanding the System: Showing how the specific technology in the case study functions (AO1).
	•	Syllabus Integration: Applying core concepts (e.g., Networks, Databases, ML) to the case study context (AO2).
	•	Ethical & Social Impacts: Discussing impacts specific to the scenario, such as privacy in rescue operations or bias in automated systems (AO3).
	•	Strategy Formulation: Evaluating and justifying strategies based on challenge questions and research (AO3).
	•	Challenge Questions: Investigating the two specific challenges provided for SL.
Skills for Learning
	•	Research Skills: Sourcing primary and secondary data, including research papers and real-world deployment examples.
	•	Thinking Skills: Evaluating the effectiveness of solutions within specific constraints (e.g., time-criticality, battery life, sensor quality).
Approaches to Teaching
	•	Inquiry-based: Students lead investigations into the specific emerging technology (e.g., vSLAM or GPS-denied environments).
	•	Assessment-informed: Using past subject reports to avoid "listing" technologies and instead focus on "evaluating" them.
	•	Collaborative: Group research sprints to build a shared repository of real-world examples and technical white papers.
Terminology
Pre-seen, Challenge Question, vSLAM (example), GPS-denied, Operational Timeframe, Stakeholder Impact, Technical Constraints, Strategic Justification, Synthesis.
Misconceptions
	•	Generic Responses: Students often provide general superficial knowledge rather than specific research-backed details.
	•	Ethics Focus only: Weaker responses focus solely on ethics while ignoring the technical computational complexity.
	•	Terminology: Misunderstanding terms specific to the annually changing case study (found on the final page of the IB release).
Adaptive Strategies
	•	Graphic Organisers: Provide "Challenge Matrix" sheets to help students map syllabus topics (Theme A/B) directly to Case Study components.
	•	Scaffolded Essay Planning: Use PEEAL (Point, Evidence, Explanation, Analysis, Link) structures for the 12-mark essay question.
	•	Keyword Glossaries: Create a bilingual or simplified glossary for the specific case study terminology to support EAL learners.
TOK Connections
	•	Evidence as Knowledge: In an emergency (like a factory fire), what counts as "good enough" knowledge for a robot to act?
	•	Expertise: How do we evaluate the credibility of research papers vs. corporate white papers when investigating emerging tech?
IBO Learner Profile
	•	Inquirers: Nurturing curiosity about current developments in computer science beyond the standard curriculum.
	•	Balanced: Weighing technical efficiency against human factors and safety constraints.
CAS Connections
	•	Advocacy: Presenting findings on emerging technologies to younger students to promote interest in future tech careers.
