=== HINT 1: Understanding NPC Dialogue ===

GOAL:
Let the player choose ONE response from the options the engine already displayed.

WHAT YOU RECEIVE:
- options: a list of dialogue choices (strings)

WHAT YOU RETURN:
- the 0-based index of the chosen option (0, 1, 2, ...)

NOTES:
- Do NOT print the options here (the engine already did).
- Convert the user's 1-based input to a 0-based index.
- Keep asking until the input is a valid number in range.
