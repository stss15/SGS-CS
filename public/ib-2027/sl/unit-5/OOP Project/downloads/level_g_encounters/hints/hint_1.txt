=== HINT 1: Understanding Encounters ===

GOAL:
Handle combat encounters where the player fights enemies.

TWO METHODS NEEDED:

1. choose_action(options)
   - The player picks what to do in combat
   - Returns the chosen action string

2. compute_damage(base_damage, armour)
   - Calculate actual damage after armour reduction
   - Returns the final damage number

THE FLOW:
  1. Enemy appears
  2. Each round: player.choose_action() is called
  3. When damage happens: player.compute_damage() calculates it
