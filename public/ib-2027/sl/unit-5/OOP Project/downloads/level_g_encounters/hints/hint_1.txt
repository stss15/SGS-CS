=== HINT 1: Understanding Encounters ===

GOAL:
Handle combat encounters by letting the player choose an action and by calculating damage.

TWO METHODS NEEDED:

1) choose_action(options)
   - The engine already shows the options
   - You read input and return the 0-based index

2) compute_damage(base_damage, armour)
   - Calculate final damage after armour reduction
   - Damage cannot be negative (clamp at 0)

THE FLOW:
1) Enemy appears
2) Each round: player.choose_action(...) is called
3) When damage happens: player.compute_damage(...) is called
