---------------------------------------------
WHY DEQUE?
---------------------------------------------
  - Normal list.pop(0) is SLOW (O(n))
  - deque.popleft() is FAST (O(1))
  - For queues, you need fast removal from front

---------------------------------------------
WHY FIFO?
---------------------------------------------
  enqueue(A), enqueue(B), enqueue(C)
  deque is now: [A, B, C]
  
  dequeue() returns A  (first in, first out)
  deque is now: [B, C]
