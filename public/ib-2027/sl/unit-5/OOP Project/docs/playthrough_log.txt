Three months ago, Sigma-7 Research Station went dark.

No distress calls. No automated reports. Nothing but silence from the
orbital facility that housed humanity's most classified research
programmes.

You were sent to investigate.

You don't remember the crash. You don't remember how you ended up in
the emergency airlock. But you remember your mission: find out what
happened, and get out alive.

The escape pod is offline. The station AI watches your every move.
And something in the shadows doesn't want you to leave.

Welcome to Sigma-7. Good luck.
