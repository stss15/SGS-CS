Concepts & Skills

Concept: Adaptation

Adjusting how you act online to stay safe, protect wellbeing, and respond appropriately to harmful or unsafe behaviour.

"How does Adaptation help you adjust how you act online to stay safe?"

Skill: Digital Literacy

Recognising harmful contact, understanding what safe behaviour looks like, and knowing when and how to report unsafe interactions.

"How can Digital Literacy help you recognise harmful contact and report unsafe interactions?"

Lesson Objectives

  • Define cyberbullying and harmful contact.
  • Identify warning signs that someone is behaving in an unsafe or inappropriate way online.
  • Explain what actions to take when faced with harmful or worrying online behaviour.

The Big Question

"Why do people act differently online than they do face-to-face?"

Task: Write down two behaviours you’ve seen online that you would rarely see in person.

Core Keywords

  • Cyberbullying — repeated harmful behaviour carried out through digital devices.
  • Harmful Contact — unwanted or unsafe messages or interactions from others.
  • Anonymity — hiding real identity online, which can change behaviour.
  • Escalation — when behaviour becomes more intense or unsafe over time.
  • Report & Block — digital tools used to stop harmful contact and alert trusted adults/platforms.
