## What you learned
- Physical skills: Safe handling of the MacBook, trackpad gestures, keyboard basics, and power management.
- File management: The OneDrive rule, folder structures, naming, and safe deletion.
- Application skills: Opening apps, managing windows, and choosing Word or PowerPoint.
- Communication: Writing clear emails and knowing when to use Teams or email.
- Organization: Using OneNote sections, tags, and sync status.

## Key habits to maintain
1. Always save to OneDrive, not On My Mac.
2. Check sync status before closing OneNote or your MacBook.
3. Use clear file and folder names.
4. Use core shortcuts: Cmd+C, Cmd+V, Cmd+S, Cmd+Shift+3, Cmd+Z.
5. Keep your Desktop and inbox tidy.

## Looking ahead
These digital habits will support every future Computer Science unit. The more automatic they become, the more time you have for creative and challenging tasks.
